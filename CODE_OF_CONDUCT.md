# Code of Conduct

SiteOS contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and respectfully.

## Our standards

Examples of constructive participation include:

- discussing technical ideas and construction workflows on their merits;
- giving actionable code-review feedback;
- respecting contributors with different backgrounds and experience levels;
- protecting private project, company, client, and personnel information;
- correcting mistakes transparently.

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, or deliberate disclosure of private project information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer. Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors or the project.
